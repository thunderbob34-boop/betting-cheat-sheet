Avoid List

A double-digit-leg NFL moneyline mega-parlay stacking this week's favorites (e.g. Ravens/Bucs/49ers/Seahawks/Eagles/Packers/Cowboys/Broncos MLs all on one ticket)

Tempting: Stacking 8-10 real favorites together produces a huge payout number, and each individual leg looks 'safe' on its own -- it's exactly the shape of Gus's real Sept 13 10-leg SGPx parlay (PIT/CIN/BAL/CHI/BUF/TEN MLs + two 2-pick SGPs, +9164 boosted to +13748).

Avoid: That real Sept 13 ticket lost, and the math explains why: even at 70-85% per leg, 8-10 legs multiplied together lands the combined probability in single-digit territory. This is Rule 4's whole point -- leg count has to follow probability, and double-digit legs never clear that bar no matter how good each individual pick is.

Atlanta Falcons -1.5 or moneyline (vs Carolina Panthers) as the 'safe favorite' pick of the closest game on the board
