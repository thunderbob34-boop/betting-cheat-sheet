The 7 ground rules

  1. Bankroll: $50 for the season plus winnings. Weekly budget: $5 total, across NFL + CFB combined.
  2. Pre-kickoff only. Built Friday night (CFB) and Saturday night (NFL). No live-bet recommendations.
  3. Bet first, boost second. The ticket is picked on merit, then checked against boosts — never built to fit one.
  4. Leg count follows probability. Parlays only when every leg is a strong favorite; coin-flip legs cap a ticket at 3.
  5. Every odds number shows its implied probability. No guessing what an odds line means.
  6. Straight bets are the default. One straight bet plus at most one small parlay per week.
  7. Claude never places a bet. This page is advice only; Gus places every bet himself in the app.
